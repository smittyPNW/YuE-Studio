YuE Studio

For the love of music.

Make yourself at home.

  1. Drag YuE Studio into Applications. If you already have an edition installed, close it and preserve its app bundle before replacing it.
  2. Open YuE Studio from Applications, then eject this disk image. Pin that installed app in the Dock to keep its icon and launch path.
  3. Choose Master to import a recording. The mastering engine, Smart Master, styles and HiFi are included. No Python setup or model download is needed for mastering.

Ready to create a song?

Generation needs a separate, one-time Python and YuE2 model setup. If your Mac already has a working YuE Studio runtime, the app uses it automatically.

On a new Mac, follow the generation setup guide. It requires an internet connection and several GB of disk space. A missing-runtime message means this setup is still needed; mastering remains available.

Generation retains full-quality GPU MLX synthesis. Generation and mastering take turns. Your songs, imported originals and saved masters are kept outside the application bundle.

Your Mac

Apple Silicon (M-series), macOS 14 or later. Full-song generation has been tested on an M4 Pro with 24 GB memory. This download does not support Intel Macs.
