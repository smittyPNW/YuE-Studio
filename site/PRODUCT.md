# YuE Studio website
<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform
web

## Stack
Static HTML, CSS and JavaScript, a dependency-free Node build script, deployed on Netlify. Selected under the owner's implementation delegation; no client framework is needed for this download page.

## Users
The AI music community, especially people with Apple Silicon Macs who want local song generation or independent mastering.

## Product Purpose
Help visitors understand YuE Studio, download its DMG, follow generation setup and find the public GitHub project. The owner is sharing the project for love of music and AI.

## Capabilities and Constraints
YuE Studio is a native macOS 14+ Apple Silicon app. Version 0.4.0 has a Developer ID signed, notarized DMG. Studio Mastering is included and works without models. Generation needs separate one-time Python/model setup; the current app does not automatically download models. Existing runtimes are reused. No paid cloud service or account is required by the app. Full quality remains full composition planning and 32-step GPU MLX synthesis. Generation and mastering are mutually exclusive. Mastering is optional and preserves originals. HiFi is an editable starting point, not a guaranteed improvement. Model weights retain their separate noncommercial license; do not invent commercial-use rights.

## Brand Commitments
YuE Studio, existing orange tuning-fork icon, burnt orange, ivory and charcoal. Music and welcoming experience come first. Use Studio Mastering, not ReSoul branding. Attribution belongs in normal page credits, not the title.

No public listening samples have been authorized or supplied for this page. Never fabricate testimonials, adoption metrics or comparisons to commercial music services.

## Product Principles
Quality over speed. Honest setup expectations. Optional mastering. Source availability and proper upstream credit. Keyboard-accessible and readable mobile/desktop website.
